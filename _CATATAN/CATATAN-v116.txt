PNW TOOLS v116 (label v9.16) — DUAL FREE CANVAS
================================================
Preview Dual diubah dari split 50/50 menjadi free canvas ala Figma.

Fitur:
- Pan: drag area kosong atau Space+drag.
- Zoom: Ctrl/Cmd/Alt+wheel, tombol +/-/100%, Fit All.
- Grid bergerak, minimap, Reset, dan Maximize.
- Frame: Edit Preview, Live Output, Stage Display, Remote Control,
  serta Browser/URL custom.
- Tambah, keluarkan, duplicate, rename, lock, bring-to-front, pop-out.
- Drag frame, resize bebas atau rasio 16:9; Alt melepas rasio sementara.
- Arrow nudge 1 px; Shift+Arrow 10 px; Ctrl/Cmd+D duplicate; Delete remove.
- Layout, daftar frame, pan/zoom, dan grid tersimpan di
  localStorage pnwCastflowDualCanvas.v1.
- Edit/Live biasa tidak berubah.
- Guard anti-rekursi pada mode display/stage/remote.

- js/cf-dual-canvas.js
- css/cf-v116.css
- CATATAN-v116.txt
